Welcome

All the office bearers of Ramesh Patel Vidya Sankul, along with the Principal, teaching staff, and administrative staff, warmly welcome you to Shri Janki Vallabh Arts and Shri M. S. Patel Commerce College.

Our college is dedicated to bringing quality higher education to students, especially those from rural and underdeveloped areas. We focus on developing students’ knowledge, discipline, cultural values, and moral character. We also strive to keep up with changing global trends and provide a positive environment for learning and personal growth.

College Trust
Our aim is to build educated, responsible, skilled, and cultured citizens by providing quality education

Vision

To provide value-based and holistic education that helps students become responsible and ideal citizens of tomorrow.

Mission

To help students gain knowledge, skills, and confidence so they can face future challenges and opportunities successfully.

Management Trust

Ramesh Patel Vidya Sankul is dedicated to providing quality education and shaping the future of students through knowledge, values, and overall development. The trust proudly manages undergraduate and postgraduate programs and aims to create a learning environment where students can grow academically and personally. With a focus on education, discipline, and skill development, we strive to prepare students for a successful future and responsible citizenship.

  • Governance with student-first values
  • Financial accountability and scholarship support
  • Value-Based Learning Environment
  • Community Engagement and Social Responsibility
  • Focus on Knowledge, Skills & Student Development
